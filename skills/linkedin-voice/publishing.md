# Publishing and measuring posts

Use when the user asks how often to post, when to post, or how a post did.

## How often

LinkedIn suggests two to five posts per week. A manageable experiment: two posts a week for a month.

## Testing

- Record results at the same age for every post, for example seven days after publishing.
- Change one thing at a time (time of day, or format). Keep subject and audience similar.

## Which number to look at

Pick the measure that answers the question the post was for:

| Goal of the post | Look at |
|---|---|
| Practical tip or checklist | saves and sends |
| Discussion | distinct readers who offered a useful example (not raw comment count) |
| Professional visibility | profile views and follows attributed to the post |

## Replying to comments

Buffer's study of about 72,000 posts found author replies were associated with roughly 30% higher
engagement, comparing posts from the same account. Caveat: comments count toward that measure, and busy
threads may attract more replies in the first place. Reply because it improves the conversation.
